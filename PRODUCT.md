# GaadiHisaab

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, TypeScript and Vite with Tailwind, shadcn/ui and GSAP.

## Users

Four equal business users at Icchamani Construction, using iPhones on site. A separate developer account provides technical administration, not business adjudication.

## Product Purpose

Track work for two Hyvas and one JCB, client payments, vehicle expenses and manual ledgers. Reuse familiar details to reduce typing. The full agreed scope lives in briefing.md.

## Operating Context

Hyvas carry material and record quantity/unit and routes. JCB jobs record hours and sites. Amounts are manual. Connectivity can be poor. Records need transparent history and review by involved users.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The production app uses real records, Clerk authentication and Supabase sync. Both light and dark mode, readable responsive controls and reduced motion are required.

## Brand Commitments

Name: GaadiHisaab. Business: Icchamani Construction. The user selected a warm flight-booking reference with rounded light surfaces, crisp hierarchy, restrained peach/pink gradients, strong actions and a compact floating navigation dock. Preserve that direction in both themes.

## Evidence on Hand

briefing.md contains the agreed discussion.

## Product Principles

Fast entry, clear state, manual control of money, reliable history, and useful suggestions based on familiar information.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch-first, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, reduced motion and readable contrast in both themes. Native iPhone conventions where appropriate without pretending the browser is a native app.
